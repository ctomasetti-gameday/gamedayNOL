Nights of Lights has a new home at the Warren County Fairgrounds!

Open Nightly November 20 through January 2

Cincinnati's favorite drive-through holiday light show has a NEW home! Nights of Lights is bringing millions of dazzling lights synchronized to your favorite holiday music to the Warren County Fairgrounds in Lebanon, Ohio, now through January 2!


Have family coming to town for Thanksgiving? Make Nights of Lights part of your holiday weekend plans! Load up the car with family and friends, turn up the holiday music, and enjoy a magical evening together, all from the comfort of your car.

Whether it's a favorite family tradition or your first visit, there's a whole new season of holiday magic waiting for you!

NEW THIS YEAR!

After experiencing millions of dazzling lights, keep the festive fun going with a stop at Santa's Village, located inside the Event Center at the Warren County Fairgrounds!

All Nights of Lights ticketholders are invited to step out of their cars and enjoy even more holiday cheer:
Meet Santa!
Say hello to the man in red and capture a magical holiday photo.
Sip & Savor!
Warm up with hot chocolate and enjoy delicious food and festive treats from food trucks.
Shop for the Holidays!
Browse local vendors offering holiday gifts and seasonal finds, perfect for checking a few names off your Christmas list!
